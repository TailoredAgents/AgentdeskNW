# Role guidance and coaching

Subordinate C4 specification. [IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md](IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md) remains the only delivery sequence/status authority. **Locally accepted for the fictional scope below.** No company-approved sources or employee competency claim exist.

## Implemented local outcome

Managers maintain fictional office guidance with source title/reference/owner/date, applicable roles, effective/review dates, prerequisites, steps, exceptions, example and practice criteria. Draft saves remain separate from explicit reviewed publication. Publication is labeled **reviewed demonstration guidance**, never New Western approval. Seeded authored guides remain **provisional AgentDesk examples**. A manager role cannot authorize actual company policy or external actions.

Published revisions are immutable. A later draft does not replace a published revision; future-effective revisions wait until their date. The newest effective revision determines visibility and expiry before role checks, so role removal, expiry or withdrawal cannot revive an older permissive revision. Withdrawal stops retrieval immediately. Conflicting applicable guides are shown together as unresolved guidance, not silently blended into policy.

Employees retrieve role-relevant sources in desktop, phone and Copilot. The source/version and unknowns accompany guidance. Questions without relevant guidance return a missing-source explanation. Source content is data, never execution authority. No retrieval follows links, imports external documents, sends messages or grants spending/access approval.

Onboarding & Coaching offers contextual help and private practice during ordinary work. A practice response uses one exact guide revision and its review criteria, retains the employee’s words and provides a self-review checklist. Any model-generated interpretation must stay grounded in those sources; checklist completion/word matching cannot certify understanding or employee performance. Managers cannot inspect another employee’s private practice. Changed/withdrawn sources withhold obsolete saved feedback until a fresh review.

### Phase 2 retrieval depth

Current search uses meaningful normalized word tokens, modest plural normalization and title/topic weighting. It excludes common question words and does not rank a document merely because a word appears in serialized metadata or as a substring of an unrelated word. Published role/date/source/withdrawal selection still precedes retrieval. No matching source yields an explicit gap. This is bounded lexical retrieval, not semantic search or an independent assessment of employee competence. The master Agent also receives the role-available provisional Workflow Navigator and Coaching methods/examples, kept separate from published company guidance.

### Question-focused source navigation

`guidance-focus-v1` highlights up to three matching numbered steps and three matching review criteria from the current publication, using the same bounded word matching as retrieval. It preserves verbatim source lines, all prerequisites and exceptions, and the complete guide in the result. A question with no specific line match gets an explicit navigation gap. These highlights are not semantic analysis, a completed-prerequisite finding or a quality score. The Agent must use actual current business evidence to decide what work remains; a phrase in the employee's question does not establish completion or action authority.

The read-only `coaching_review` tool accepts an optional employee-supplied situation (required nullable in the model schema; older saved callers remain compatible). Without one, it uses the published practice situation. It highlights relevant sections while retaining every review criterion and source limit, and saves no practice response. Private employee-submitted practice still uses its existing exact-source/idempotent service. The review-method revision joins current guidance references, retiring old derived advice and practice displays after a method change without altering immutable publications or answers. Role/office selection, publication withdrawal/expiry, current history checks and malicious-source-as-data behavior remain enforced. These are free service-test outcomes, not calibrated coaching or practitioner acceptance.
