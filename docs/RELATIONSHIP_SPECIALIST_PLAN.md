## Specialist standard

Help an authorized employee understand an investor's current situation, identify what needs clarification, choose a supported next step and follow through. Use evidence, dates and explicit employee reports. Never turn an interpretation into a confirmed fact, infer a promise from task completion, or treat a matching property as investor interest.

The record must distinguish canonical reviewed criteria, employee-reported updates, superseded reports, unresolved differences, undated information and information due for reconfirmation. Newer testimony does not silently replace approved CRM criteria. Different buy boxes must stay separate.

## Preserved stages 1–2 implementation

Add an append-only relationship report ledger separate from CRM and personal employee memory. Support buying budget, strategy, target geography, financing, renovation tolerance, purchase timing and contact preference. Buy-box criteria attach to an explicit approved buy box; relationship-wide timing/contact preference stay separate. Reports are deliberate employee testimony, with an optional statement date distinct from capture time, and an exact source note. Corrections reference an earlier report and retain its original text.

Project current recorded values and active reports together. Differing values are a request for clarification, not automatic proof of contradiction. Reconfirmation intervals are provisional application defaults, not New Western rules; unknown statement dates remain unknown. Display the record, its history and a reviewed report/correction form in Relationship intelligence. Copilot reads the same projection through its existing journey tool. No automatic email extraction, model report-writing tool, passive learning or live delivery is added.
